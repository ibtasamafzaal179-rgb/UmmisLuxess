Our Heritage

Slow craft,
patient beauty

Every UmmisLuxe fragrance begins in a small atelier, where raw oils are blended by hand and left to rest. We do not rush maceration. We do not chase trends. We make perfumes the old way — for the few who understand that luxury is never loud.

From the rose fields of the Mediterranean to the oud forests of Southeast Asia, we source ingredients with respect for the people and the land behind them.

  • 40+days of maturation
  • 100%natural essential oils
  • Handblended in small batches

A Message from the CEO

"Perfume is memory you can wear. When I founded UmmisLuxe, I wanted to bottle the feeling of home — the rosewater my grandmother pressed by hand, the Hayaati that lingered in our doorways, the warmth of being remembered. Every fragrance we make is a quiet letter to the women who taught me that elegance is not seen first, but felt."

Aisha Aminu

Founder & Chief Executive Officer, UmmisLuxe
